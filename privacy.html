T Wraps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

T Wraps ("the app") lets you design wrap images for your car's 3D visualization. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

  • Your designs, exported wrap images and settings are stored only on your device (app storage and a local database). They are not uploaded to us.
  • Photos you add to a design are copied into the app's private storage. The app only accesses photos you pick in the system photo picker.
  • Exported images are saved to your photo library, shared, or copied to a folder only when you choose to do so.

We do not operate servers and do not collect your designs, photos or files.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and interactions with ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • iOS: the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with applicable privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings in the app.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Third-party content

Vehicle templates and example wraps are based on public custom-wraps resources. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.
